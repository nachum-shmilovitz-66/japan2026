      Prices are the Booking.com totals for two guests, tax and service charge included; the dollar figures are the estimates printed on each confirmation and will move with the rate, since every property is paid in yen on arrival. Drive times assume expressway routing and no stops — add tolls and the Japanese habit of a service-area break. Nothing cancels itself: each losing reservation has to be cancelled through Booking.com or it charges as a no-show. Reservation numbers and PINs are not printed in this copy of the page — the confirmations themselves print only your.booking.com, so the links are assembled rather than copied; the lookup form does not take the number from the link, so tap “copy number” first and paste it in. On a phone each link opens the web lookup in a new tab — Android hands it to the Booking.com app by itself when the app is installed — and a small “in the app” link beside it asks for the app explicitly: it opens the app on its Bookings tab, where all seventeen are listed — Booking.com gives outside links no way to land on one specific booking.
